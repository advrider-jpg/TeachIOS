# Screenshot Copy Plan

The generated PNGs in `docs/release/markforme_release_package/03_screenshots/templates/` are layout templates only. Final App Store screenshots must be captured from the real app.

Do not show real student names, real school names, real teacher names, real student work, real addresses or real email addresses. Use fake sample data only.

## Required screenshot order

1. Mark student work faster
2. Use your rubric or marking guide
3. Check the scanned text
4. Check the evidence first
5. Edit marks and feedback
6. Nothing is final until you approve it
7. Export feedback when ready
8. Student work is not sent to us

## Screenshot guidance

- Use fake names such as Ava, Noah, Mia and Lucas.
- Use fake class names such as Year 7 English or Year 5 Writing.
- Use simple written-response examples.
- Do not imply official curriculum endorsement.
- Do not say marking is fully automatic.
- Replace the templates with real iPhone and iPad screenshots before App Store upload.
