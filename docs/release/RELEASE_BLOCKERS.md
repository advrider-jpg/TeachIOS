# Release Blockers

These items must be completed before App Store submission.

## App identity and legal

- Confirm final app name.
- Check App Store Connect name availability.
- Check trade mark and domain risk separately.
- Confirm final legal owner and copyright.
- Final legal review of privacy and education claims.

## URLs and support

- Replace placeholder support URL: https://markforme.app/support
- Replace placeholder privacy policy URL: https://markforme.app/privacy
- Replace placeholder marketing URL if used: https://markforme.app
- Publish the support page.
- Publish the privacy policy.
- Replace contact placeholders, including `replace@example.com`, phone/contact method and review contact details.

## Apple setup

- Confirm bundle ID and App ID. Current repo release setting: `com.markforme.app`.
- Configure signing and Apple Developer Team.
- Complete App Store privacy answers.
- Complete age rating questionnaire.
- Decide category and availability.
- Complete any App Review contact details.
- Run Xcode archive.
- Upload to TestFlight.

## Screenshots and assets

- Add screenshots captured from the real app.
- Replace screenshot templates with real screenshots.
- Confirm the app icon renders in Xcode, TestFlight and Archive.

## Device and privacy testing

- Test on physical devices.
- Test camera scan.
- Test import and export.
- Test data deletion.
- Test backup and restore.
- Test no-network behaviour if privacy claims rely on it.
- Verify no analytics, tracking, cloud sync, cloud OCR, cloud marking, crash reporting or developer-accessible telemetry if claiming Data Not Collected.
- Verify all third-party SDK privacy manifests.

## Curriculum and school review

- Review curriculum attribution and rights.
- Confirm MarkForMe does not claim ACARA approval, government approval, school-system approval or guaranteed curriculum compliance.
- Confirm school-policy wording with intended users.
