# Sources and requirements used

## Apple requirements checked

- App name: 2 to 30 characters.
- Subtitle: up to 30 characters.
- Privacy policy URL: required for iOS apps.
- Promotional text: up to 170 characters.
- Description: up to 4,000 characters.
- Keywords: up to 100 bytes and should not duplicate app or company names.
- Screenshots: required, one to ten screenshots, .jpg, .jpeg or .png.
- iPhone 6.9 inch screenshots: accepted portrait sizes include 1260 × 2736, 1290 × 2796 and 1320 × 2868.
- iPad 13 inch screenshots: required if the app runs on iPad; accepted portrait sizes include 2064 × 2752 and 2048 × 2732.
- Support URL: required and should lead to actual contact information.
- App Review notes should include information needed to test the app.

## Australian school trust points used

- The Australian Framework for Generative AI in Schools supports responsible and ethical use of AI in schools and is organised around principles including accountability, privacy, security and safety.
- Victorian school guidance says teacher professional judgement should stay central for assessment and feedback decisions, and AI output should be reviewed by a human before decisions are made.
- The repo's own privacy review says the app currently has no developer-accessible data transmission, analytics, accounts, cloud OCR, cloud marking or support upload.

## Repo facts used

- Teacher-controlled marking for text-based student work.
- Scan/import/paste/PDF input.
- Teacher OCR/text review before marking.
- Rubrics, answer keys, exemplars and teacher instructions.
- Australian Curriculum reference catalog with non-endorsement warnings.
- Draft marks and feedback where supported.
- Teacher final review and approval.
- Student PDF, teacher record, CSV, ZIP/archive and backup exports.
- No backend, no account login, no analytics SDK and no network entitlement request in the current repo posture.
