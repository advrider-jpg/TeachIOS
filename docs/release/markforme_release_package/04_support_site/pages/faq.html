FAQ

Does MarkForMe replace the teacher?

No. Teachers check the student work, review the evidence, edit marks and feedback, and approve the final result.

What student work is it best for?

It is best for text-based student work, written responses, short-answer tasks and rubric-based assessments.

Can it mark handwriting?

The app can scan paper work and help turn it into text. The teacher should check the text before marking starts.

Does it send student work to MarkForMe?

No. Student work is not sent to us for marking.

Can I use my own rubric?

Yes. You can use a rubric, marking guide, success criteria or answer key.

Does it claim official curriculum approval?

No. Any Australian Curriculum references are reference aids. Teachers choose what to use and must follow school policy.
