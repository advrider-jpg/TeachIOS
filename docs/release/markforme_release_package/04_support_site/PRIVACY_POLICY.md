## Summary

Student work is not sent to us for marking.

Student work stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

## Information the app may handle

MarkForMe may handle student names, class names, assignment names, scanned student work, imported PDFs or images, pasted student work, scanned or extracted text, rubrics, marking guides, criteria, answer keys, exemplars, task instructions, teacher notes, mapped curriculum references, draft marks and feedback, final marks and feedback, student feedback, teacher records, gradebooks, archives and backups.

## Where information is stored

According to the current repo, the app stores its core records locally on the device. Local files may be included in device backups depending on device, account, school and mobile-device-management settings.

## What is sent to the developer

The current repo does not send student work, student names, class records, rubrics, marks, feedback, teacher notes, exports or backups to the developer for marking.

## Accounts, ads and tracking

The current repo does not require an account and does not include ads, subscriptions, in-app purchases, analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync or developer-accessible telemetry in the core app path.

## Exports and sharing

Teachers can choose to export student feedback, teacher records, gradebooks, archives or backups. Once exported or shared, those files are outside the app and should be handled under school policy.

## Deleting data

Use the app's delete controls where provided. Delete exported files and backups wherever they were saved or shared. Removing the app from a device removes app data stored on that device.

## School policy

Always follow your school's policy before entering student information into any app.
