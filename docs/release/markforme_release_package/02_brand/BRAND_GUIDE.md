# MarkForMe brand guide

## Brand idea

MarkForMe is teacher marking help.

The tone is plain, direct and practical. It should sound like a useful teaching tool, not an AI product pitch.

## Name

**MarkForMe**

Use this exact casing in marketing copy, support pages and the App Store.

## App Store subtitle

**Teacher marking help**

## Main line

**Mark student work. Check the result.**

## Short lines

- Mark student work faster.
- Use your rubric.
- Check the evidence.
- Review, edit and approve.
- Student work is not sent to us.

## What to avoid

Avoid these phrases in public marketing:

- local-first
- on-device
- autonomous grading
- AI-powered marking
- workflow engine
- human-in-the-loop
- OCR validation
- compliance engine
- curriculum-aligned intelligence

## Icon recommendation

Use the primary sheet-and-check icon for the first release because it is simple and already close to the repo's current visual language. It says marking, student work and completion without adding a cartoon teacher, robot or AI symbol.

## Image use rules

Keep screenshots clean. Use fake student work. Avoid real student names, school logos and third-party logos.

Do not use the generated screenshot templates as final App Store screenshots unless the app UI shown inside the frame is replaced with a real capture from the app.
