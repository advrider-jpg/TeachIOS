# Release Blockers

These items must be completed before TestFlight or App Store submission. Owner, date, and evidence stay explicit so the release package cannot look complete while required proof is missing.

| ID | Area | Status | Owner | Evidence date | Required evidence | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RB-001 | App identity and legal | Blocked | Release owner | Not recorded | Final public app name, legal owner, copyright, App Store Connect name availability, trade mark/domain review, and legal review of privacy/education claims. | Identity and legal review are recorded with owner/date and no open legal release questions. |
| RB-002 | Support and privacy URLs | Blocked | Release owner | Not recorded | Real support URL and real privacy policy URL controlled by the release owner. Marketing URL only if a real marketing site is live. | Working URLs are recorded in App Store metadata, support-site package, and App Review notes. |
| RB-003 | Contact details | Blocked | Release owner | Not recorded | Real support contact method plus App Review contact name and phone/email. | Contact details are present in release docs and no support/contact blocker text remains. |
| RB-004 | Package resolution | Blocked | macOS/Xcode release engineer | Not recorded | `xcodebuild -resolvePackageDependencies -project GradeDraft.xcodeproj -scheme GradeDraft` output and committed `Package.resolved`. | `Package.resolved` exists in the repo and release readiness no longer fails this gate. |
| RB-005 | Xcode build/test validation | Blocked | macOS/Xcode release engineer | Not recorded | Passing Xcode build, deterministic unit tests, SwiftLint, UI smoke, screenshot smoke, and unsigned Release build evidence. | Validation commands and artifacts are dated in `VALIDATION_LEDGER.md` or release QA docs. |
| RB-006 | Signing and App Store Connect | Blocked | Release owner | Not recorded | Production signing/team configuration, App Store Connect app record, privacy answers, age rating, category/availability, archive, TestFlight upload. | Signed archive/export and TestFlight upload evidence are recorded. |
| RB-007 | Screenshots and assets | Blocked | Release owner | Not recorded | Real screenshots captured from the app and app icon verification in Xcode/TestFlight/archive. | Screenshot set and icon validation evidence are dated and linked. |
| RB-008 | Manual simulator/device QA | Blocked | QA owner | Not recorded | Dated simulator and physical-device runs for camera scan, OCR, PDF import/export, share sheet, local auth, data deletion, backup/restore, Airplane Mode, and Foundation Models availability behavior where supported. | `MANUAL_QA_RESULTS.md` contains real dated results and release readiness no longer fails the manual-QA gate. |
| RB-009 | Privacy and no-network verification | Blocked | Privacy/release owner | Not recorded | Final binary review for no analytics, tracking, cloud sync, cloud OCR, cloud marking, crash reporting, developer telemetry, and third-party SDK privacy manifests. | Privacy review is dated against the final binary and App Store privacy answers match the evidence. |
| RB-010 | Curriculum and school review | Blocked | Curriculum/release owner | Not recorded | Curriculum attribution/rights review, school-policy wording review, no-endorsement claim review, and device performance evidence for the indexed local curriculum store. | Review outcomes and performance evidence are recorded with owner/date. |
