## Long description

MarkForMe is teacher marking help.

Add student work by scanning, importing or pasting.

Use your rubric, marking guide, criteria, answer key or task instructions. MarkForMe can help draft marks and feedback, but the result is not final.

Check the scanned text before marking. Check the evidence from the student's work. Edit marks and feedback before anything is final.

Nothing is final until you approve it.

Export student feedback or a teacher record when ready.

Student work is not sent to us for marking. Student work stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

Always follow your school's policy before entering student information into any app.

MarkForMe helps with marking. It does not replace teacher judgement.
