## Intro

MarkForMe helps teachers mark text-based student work. This page explains the main steps and how to get help.

## Main steps

1. Create a class and assignment.
2. Add student work by scanning, importing or pasting it.
3. Add a rubric, marking guide, success criteria or answer key.
4. Check the scanned or extracted text.
5. Review draft marks and feedback, or mark manually.
6. Edit marks and feedback.
7. Approve the final result.
8. Export feedback or a teacher record when ready.

## Privacy line

Student work is not sent to us for marking. It stays on your device unless you export or share it.
