## Summary

Student work is not sent to us for marking.

MarkForMe stores student work, class records, rubrics, marks, feedback and teacher notes on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

## What the app handles

The app may handle:

- student names;
- class names;
- assignment names;
- scanned student work;
- imported PDFs or images;
- pasted student work;
- scanned or extracted text;
- rubrics, marking guides, success criteria, answer keys, exemplars and teacher instructions;
- mapped curriculum references;
- draft marks and feedback;
- final marks and feedback;
- private teacher notes;
- exported reports, gradebooks, archives and backups.

## What is sent to us

The current app does not send student work, student names, class records, rubrics, marks, feedback, teacher notes, exports or backups to us.

## Accounts

The current app does not require an account.

## Ads and tracking

The current app does not include ads or tracking.

## Exports and sharing

Teachers can choose to export student feedback, teacher records, gradebooks, archives or backups. Once exported or shared, those files are outside the app and should be handled under school policy.

## Deleting data

Delete records inside the app where deletion controls are provided. Delete exported files and backups wherever they were saved or shared. Removing the app from a device removes app data stored on that device.

## School policy

Teachers should follow their school policy before entering student information into any app.
