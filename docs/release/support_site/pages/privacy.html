Privacy

Student work is not sent to us for marking.

MarkForMe stores student work, class records, rubrics, marks, feedback and teacher notes on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Information handled by the app

The app may store student names, class names, assignment names, scanned student work, imported files, pasted text, rubrics, answer keys, teacher instructions, draft feedback, final marks, teacher notes, exports and backups.

Information sent to us

The current app does not send student work, rubrics, marks, feedback, teacher notes or reports to us.

Accounts, ads and tracking

The current app does not require an account. It does not include advertising or tracking.

Exports and sharing

If you export or share a report, gradebook, archive or backup, that file leaves the app. You are responsible for where you save or send it.

Deleting data

You can delete records in the app where deletion controls are provided. You can also remove the app from your device to delete app data stored on that device. Exported files and backups need to be deleted wherever you saved or shared them.

School policy

Always follow your school policy before entering student information into any app.
