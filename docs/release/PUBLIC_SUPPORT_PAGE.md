# Mark My Work Support Page

Mark My Work is a local-first teacher grading assistant for scanning, importing, reviewing, drafting, finalizing, and exporting feedback for text-based student work.

## Before contacting support
Please do not send student work, student names, grades, private teacher notes, screenshots containing student records, or exported archives. Describe the issue using anonymous examples.

## Common checks

### The app cannot generate a local AI draft
Local AI drafting depends on device and operating-system support. Confirm that the device supports Apple Intelligence, that Apple Intelligence is enabled, and that the on-device model is ready. When local AI is unavailable, Mark My Work does not use a cloud fallback. Manual final review remains available.

### Student export is blocked
Student-facing reports are blocked until the teacher approves a current final review. If student work, rubric text, answer key, exemplar, evidence, or grading instructions changed after a draft or final review was created, recheck the draft or final review before exporting.

### OCR text needs correction
Open the OCR review workflow, inspect the extracted lines against the source image or PDF render, correct or reject uncertain lines, and mark the document reviewed before drafting feedback.

### Sensitive export requires authentication
Teacher audit exports, archives, backups, and gradebook exports can contain student records or private teacher notes. Confirm the export warning and complete Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode authentication where available.

### Backup restore should be previewed first
Use the restore preview before importing a backup. Choose whether to restore as copy, keep local records, or replace local records only after reviewing the preview.

## Privacy reminder
Mark My Work is designed so the core workflow remains on the device. Support channels must not be used to transmit student records unless the publisher later creates and documents a legally reviewed secure support process.
