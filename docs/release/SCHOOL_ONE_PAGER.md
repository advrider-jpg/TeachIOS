# MarkForMe For Schools

## What MarkForMe does

MarkForMe helps teachers mark text-based student work.

Teachers add student work by scanning, importing or pasting. They add a rubric or marking guide, check the scanned text, check evidence from the student's work, edit marks and feedback, approve the final result, and export feedback or a teacher record when ready.

## What it does not do

- It does not replace teacher judgement.
- It does not make a final decision without the teacher.
- It does not claim official curriculum approval.
- It does not guarantee school policy compliance.
- It does not replace moderation, school reporting requirements, or professional judgement.
- It is not for posters, models, visual art, complex diagrams or symbolic maths unless the teacher confirms the text evidence.

## Teacher control

Nothing is final until the teacher approves it.

## Student data

Student work is not sent to us for marking. Student work stays on the device unless the teacher chooses to export or share it.

Teachers should always follow their school's policy before entering student information into any app.

## Best fit

MarkForMe is best suited for written responses, short-answer work, rubric tasks, marking-guide tasks, student feedback and teacher records.
