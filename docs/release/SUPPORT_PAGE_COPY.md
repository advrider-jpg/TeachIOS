## Heading

MarkForMe Support

## Intro

MarkForMe helps teachers mark text-based student work. It does not replace teacher judgement.

## Main steps

1. Create a class and assignment.
2. Add student work by scanning, importing or pasting.
3. Add a rubric, marking guide, criteria, answer key or task instructions.
4. Check the scanned text before marking.
5. Check the evidence from the student's work.
6. Review draft marks and feedback, or mark manually.
7. Edit marks and feedback.
8. Approve the final result.
9. Export student feedback or a teacher record when ready.

## Draft feedback is not available on this device

If draft feedback is not available on this device, continue with manual marking and final review. MarkForMe does not send student work away for marking as a fallback.

## Delete local data

Use the delete controls in the app where provided. Delete exported files, teacher records, archives and backups wherever you saved or shared them.

## Privacy

Student work is not sent to us for marking. Student work stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it. Always follow your school's policy before entering student information into any app.
