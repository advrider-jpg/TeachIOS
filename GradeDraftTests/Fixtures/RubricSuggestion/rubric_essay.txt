Persuasive essay rubric

Thesis — 4 marks: clear position stated in the introduction and held throughout the essay. A strong response (4 marks) states an arguable position; a developing response (2 marks) names the topic but not a position; a limited response (1 mark) has no clear position.

Evidence — 6 marks: each main point is supported by relevant quotations or examples from the novel, explained in the student's own words. Strong (5–6 marks): every point is supported and explained. Developing (3–4 marks): most points are supported but some evidence is not explained. Limited (1–2 marks): little or no supporting evidence.

Structure and expression — 5 marks: paragraphs follow a logical order with clear topic sentences, and the writing uses accurate spelling, punctuation and grammar. Strong (5 marks), sound (3–4 marks), limited (1–2 marks).
