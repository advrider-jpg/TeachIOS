Short answer question: Why do we have seasons?

Accuracy — 3 marks: correctly explains that the Earth's axis is tilted and that this changes how directly sunlight reaches each hemisphere during the year. 3 marks for a complete and correct explanation, 2 marks for a partly correct explanation, 1 mark for a relevant but incorrect idea.

Explanation — 2 marks: links the tilt to longer days and more direct sunlight in summer, using one clear example. 2 marks for a clear link with an example, 1 mark for a link without an example.
